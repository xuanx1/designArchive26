Vessel — Generative Olfactory

Vessel is a generative design tool that transforms a perfumer's formula into a 3D bottle in real time. Users compose fragrances across olfactory families, note structure, and concentration, and the system parametrically derives the physical flacon — silhouette, cross-section, surface relief, Venetian decoration, glass tint, hardware, and label typography — from the underlying formula data.

Direct editing of dimensions and the CMF palette lets users fine-tune the derived design; drydown simulation, shelf comparison against reference houses, and shareable design URLs turn each formula into a portable artefact. The tool sits between industrial design, data visualisation, and olfactory storytelling — treating perfume itself as the source code for its bottle.
